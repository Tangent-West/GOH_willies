PLAN. DON’T BAN.
PROTECT FARMERS. PROTECT FREEDOMS.

Hey there,

Before hemp becomes something on a shelf, it starts with people willing to plant a crop and see it through.

Farmers make decisions seasons ahead. The businesses that process and make hemp products need rules they can build around. Right now, federal uncertainty makes that harder.

The Goodness of Hemp® is asking for a workable future across the plant, from fiber and grain to responsibly regulated consumer products.

That means clear rules, consumer protections, and room for American agriculture to grow. It also means protecting the freedom of adults to choose responsibly.

Willie has stood with family farmers for decades. Let’s stand with the people growing hemp today. Review the message and tell Congress you support the Goodness of Hemp before December 11.
