PLAN. DON’T BAN.
HERE’S TO AMERICAN FARMERS.

Hey there,

Before the table fills and the music starts, here’s to the people who grow the good things we get to share.

Supporting American farmers has long been part of Willie’s story. This Thanksgiving week, we’re asking our community to show up for hemp farmers and the businesses that help bring this plant into everyday life.

December 11 is getting closer. Congress still has an opportunity to act, and we want the Farm Bill and other hemp legislation to reflect thoughtful policy that supports agriculture and protects consumers.

If you haven’t sent your message yet, this is a good moment. If you have, thank you. Share the campaign with someone who cares about American farmers and responsible choice.
