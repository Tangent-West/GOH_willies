PLAN. DON’T BAN.
10 DAYS. ADD YOUR VOICE.

Hey there,

There are ten days until December 11. We’re asking everyone who cares about hemp’s future to make their voice heard.

Federal changes scheduled for that date could put many hemp-derived products out of reach. Farmers and responsible businesses need a practical path forward.

We want Congress to carry the Goodness of Hemp priorities into the Farm Bill or another available bill: support American agriculture, protect consumers, and keep responsible hemp products available.

There is still an opportunity to act. Please review the message, make it your own, and send it to your members of Congress today. A future for good hemp is worth speaking up for.
