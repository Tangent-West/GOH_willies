PLAN. DON’T BAN.
STANDING WITH FARMERS. SINCE 1985.

Hey there,

In 1985, Willie helped launch Farm Aid with Neil Young and John Mellencamp to stand with America’s family farmers. More than 40 years later, that commitment still runs deep.

Today, hemp farmers need our voices, too. Federal changes coming December 11 could put many hemp-derived products out of reach.

The Goodness of Hemp® brings farmers, businesses, and communities together around a practical plan: protect consumers, support American agriculture, and keep responsible hemp products available.

We’re asking Congress to carry those priorities into the Farm Bill or other legislation. There’s still an opportunity to build a better future for hemp. Let’s make sure the people who grow it, make it, and enjoy it are heard.
