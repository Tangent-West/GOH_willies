PLAN. DON’T BAN.
STANDING WITH FARMERS. SINCE 1985.

Hey there,

In 1985, Willie helped launch Farm Aid with Neil Young and John Mellencamp to stand with America’s family farmers.

More than 40 years later, that commitment still runs deep. Today, hemp farmers need our voices, too.

Federal changes coming December 11 could put many hemp-derived products out of reach. Let’s stand with the farmers and small businesses behind hemp—and protect responsible adult choice.

We’re asking Congress for clear rules that protect consumers, support American agriculture, and keep responsible hemp products available.
