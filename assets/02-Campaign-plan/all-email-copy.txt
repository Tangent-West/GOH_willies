SUBJECT: Plan. Don’t Ban. Let’s keep the good growing.
PREHEADER: Stand with American farmers and help shape hemp’s future.

PLAN. DON’T BAN.
STANDING WITH FARMERS. SINCE 1985.

Hey there,

In 1985, Willie helped launch Farm Aid with Neil Young and John Mellencamp to stand with America’s family farmers. More than 40 years later, that commitment still runs deep.

Today, hemp farmers need our voices, too. Federal changes coming December 11 could put many hemp-derived products out of reach.

The Goodness of Hemp® brings farmers, businesses, and communities together around a practical plan: protect consumers, support American agriculture, and keep responsible hemp products available.

We’re asking Congress to carry those priorities into the Farm Bill or other legislation. There’s still an opportunity to build a better future for hemp. Let’s make sure the people who grow it, make it, and enjoy it are heard.

PLAN. DON’T BAN.
PROTECT FARMERS. PROTECT FREEDOMS.

Hey there,

Before hemp becomes something on a shelf, it starts with people willing to plant a crop and see it through.

Farmers make decisions seasons ahead. The businesses that process and make hemp products need rules they can build around. Right now, federal uncertainty makes that harder.

The Goodness of Hemp® is asking for a workable future across the plant, from fiber and grain to responsibly regulated consumer products.

That means clear rules, consumer protections, and room for American agriculture to grow. It also means protecting the freedom of adults to choose responsibly.

Willie has stood with family farmers for decades. Let’s stand with the people growing hemp today. Review the message and tell Congress you support the Goodness of Hemp before December 11.
